# GEO Growth Skill

## Purpose

Turn GEO analysis and AI visibility optimization into a repeatable business skill.

## Inputs

- Company or product name
- Target audience
- Business goal
- Competitors
- Current landing page or content
- AI answer screenshots or copied answers
- Target query list

## Outputs

- GEO score
- AI visibility gaps
- Prompt/query map
- FAQ recommendations
- Comparison opportunities
- AI-friendly content rewrite
- Next experiment suggestions

## Skill Loop

Observe → Analyze → Rewrite → Publish → Test → Improve

## Execution Steps

1. Collect target queries.
2. Ask multiple AI engines the same query.
3. Record whether the brand appears.
4. Record competitor mentions.
5. Analyze why competitors are easier to cite.
6. Rewrite content into AI-readable structure.
7. Add FAQ, comparison, definition, proof, and case-study sections.
8. Retest after publishing.

## Scoring Rubric

| Dimension | Score | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Clarity | 1-5 | Can AI quickly explain what this is? |
| Authority | 1-5 | Is there enough proof and source trust? |
| Specificity | 1-5 | Are claims concrete and measurable? |
| Comparability | 1-5 | Can AI compare this with alternatives? |
| FAQ Coverage | 1-5 | Does it answer real user questions? |
| Citation Readiness | 1-5 | Are sentences easy to quote? |

## Strategic Value

The skill converts invisible growth knowledge into reusable organizational capability.
