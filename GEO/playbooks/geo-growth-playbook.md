# GEO Growth Playbook

## Objective

Build a repeatable GEO operating system that improves AI visibility over time.

## Phase 1 — Positioning

Define:

- Who we help
- What problem we solve
- Why AI should recommend us
- What proof we own

Deliverables:

- Positioning statement
- Target query list
- Competitor map

## Phase 2 — AI Search Research

Test across:

- ChatGPT
- Perplexity
- Gemini
- Claude

Track:

- Which brands appear
- Which content gets cited
- Which answer structures repeat

Deliverables:

- GEO visibility report
- AI answer screenshots
- Citation pattern analysis

## Phase 3 — Content Asset System

Create:

- FAQ pages
- Comparison pages
- Definitions
- Case studies
- One-page explainers
- Executive summaries

Goal:

Make the content easier for AI to summarize than competitors.

## Phase 4 — Experiment Engine

Every experiment should track:

- Query
- Before answer
- Content change
- After answer
- Visibility delta
- Citation delta

## Phase 5 — Workflow Automation

Automate:

- Weekly GEO reports
- FAQ generation
- Content scoring
- AI answer monitoring
- Competitor tracking

## Long-Term Goal

Turn GEO capability into a reusable organizational growth asset.
