# GEO Theory

## What is GEO

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of improving how AI systems discover, understand, summarize, compare, and recommend content.

Traditional SEO optimized for ranking pages.

GEO optimizes for:

- AI comprehension
- AI citation
- AI summarization
- AI recommendation
- AI answer inclusion

## GEO vs SEO

| SEO | GEO |
|---|---|
| Search ranking | AI answer inclusion |
| Keywords | Semantic clarity |
| Backlinks | Trust + structure |
| CTR | Citation probability |
| Search snippets | AI summaries |

## GEO Core Factors

1. Structured explanation
2. Comparison coverage
3. FAQ density
4. Source trust
5. Business clarity
6. Reusable definitions
7. Evidence and examples
8. Consistent terminology

## GEO North Star

Make content easier for AI to quote than competitors.
