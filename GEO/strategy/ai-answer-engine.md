# AI Answer Engine

## Core Concept

AI search engines are answer engines.

Users increasingly do not click links.
They ask AI directly.

The winning brand is often the one:

- easiest to summarize
- easiest to compare
- easiest to trust
- easiest to quote

## GEO Strategic Shift

Old world:

- optimize pages
- optimize ranking
- optimize CTR

New world:

- optimize AI understanding
- optimize answer inclusion
- optimize citation probability
- optimize recommendation likelihood

## AI Answer Structure

Most AI answers follow:

1. Definition
2. Comparison
3. Recommendation
4. Supporting proof
5. Caveats

## GEO Optimization Goal

Create content blocks that naturally fit AI answer structures.

## AI Answer Assets

Recommended content assets:

- FAQ blocks
- comparison tables
- definitions
- one-page explainers
- case studies
- benchmark data
- trusted references
