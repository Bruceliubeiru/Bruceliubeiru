# GEO Ranking Engine

## Purpose

Build a repeatable GEO ranking and visibility scoring system.

## Core GEO Dimensions

### 1. Semantic Clarity

Can AI quickly understand:

- what the business is
- what problem it solves
- who it serves

### 2. Citation Readiness

Can AI easily quote the content?

Signals:

- concise sentences
- clear definitions
- reusable explanations
- factual structure

### 3. Comparison Readiness

Can AI compare this solution against alternatives?

Signals:

- comparison tables
- competitor references
- differentiation clarity

### 4. FAQ Coverage

Does the content answer real user intent?

### 5. Authority Signal

Signals:

- proof points
- metrics
- benchmarks
- references
- case studies

## GEO Score Formula

Future formula:

GEO Score =
Semantic Clarity + Citation Readiness + FAQ Coverage + Authority + Comparison Readiness

## Long-Term Goal

Create measurable GEO ranking factors for AI visibility.
