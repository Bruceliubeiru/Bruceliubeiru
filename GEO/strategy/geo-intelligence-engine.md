# GEO Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

Build an intelligence layer for AI visibility.

## Core Intelligence Systems

### 1. Citation Intelligence

Track:

- when AI cites content
- what wording is reused
- which structures are preferred

### 2. Visibility Intelligence

Track:

- brand visibility
- answer inclusion
- recommendation frequency

### 3. Competitor Intelligence

Track:

- why competitors rank better
- which GEO structures they use
- FAQ and comparison gaps

### 4. Ranking Intelligence

Analyze:

- GEO score changes
- semantic clarity
- authority growth
- answer quality trends

## Long-Term Goal

Create an AI-native growth intelligence platform.
