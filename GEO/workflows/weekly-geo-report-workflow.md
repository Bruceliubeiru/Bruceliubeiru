# Weekly GEO Report Workflow

## Objective

Automatically generate weekly GEO visibility reports.

## Inputs

- AI query results
- visibility score
- citation tracking
- competitor tracking
- experiment results

## Workflow

1. Collect AI answers.
2. Score visibility.
3. Compare competitor visibility.
4. Generate GEO insights.
5. Recommend next experiments.
6. Publish weekly report.

## Future Automation

Integrations:

- OpenAI API
- Feishu
- Dify
- Email / Slack notification

## Weekly GEO KPIs

- AI mention rate
- citation growth
- FAQ coverage growth
- GEO score trend
- competitor delta
