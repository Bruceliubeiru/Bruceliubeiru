# LLM Ranking and AI Answer Visibility

## Core Idea

AI systems do not rank pages the same way traditional search engines do.

They optimize for:

- semantic clarity
- trusted sources
- reusable explanations
- concise summaries
- comparison quality
- FAQ coverage
- evidence density

## GEO Ranking Hypothesis

Higher AI visibility usually correlates with:

1. Better structure
2. Better definitions
3. Better comparison content
4. Better trust signals
5. Better answer completeness
6. Easier-to-quote language

## Strategic Shift

Traditional SEO:

- optimize clicks
- optimize keywords
- optimize pages

GEO:

- optimize AI understanding
- optimize AI summarization
- optimize AI recommendation
- optimize AI citation probability

## Long-Term Vision

Build datasets and experiments to discover repeatable GEO ranking patterns.
