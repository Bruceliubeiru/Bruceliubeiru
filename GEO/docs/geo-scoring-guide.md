# GEO Scoring Guide

## Purpose

The GEO Scoring Engine measures how AI-friendly and GEO-ready content is.

## Current Dimensions

| Dimension | Max Score |
|---|---|
| Semantic Clarity | 20 |
| Citation Readiness | 20 |
| FAQ Coverage | 15 |
| Comparison Readiness | 15 |
| Authority Signal | 15 |
| AI Readability | 15 |

## Scoring Logic

### Semantic Clarity (20)

- Clear definition: 5
- Target user or use case: 5
- Problem or value explained: 5
- Enough context: 3
- Question framing: 2

### Citation Readiness (20)

- Complete sentences: 5
- Quote-ready length: 5
- Reasoning markers: 4
- Specific claims: 4
- Concise enough: 2

### FAQ Coverage (15)

- Questions present: 5
- FAQ labels: 4
- How-to intent: 3
- Comparison intent: 3

### Comparison Readiness (15)

- Comparison wording: 5
- Alternatives: 4
- Selection logic: 4
- Table-like structure: 2

### Authority Signal (15)

- Data or metrics: 4
- Examples or cases: 4
- Numbers present: 3
- Evidence language: 4
- Trust or policy clarity: 4

The dimension is capped at 15 points.

### AI Readability (15)

- Headings or markdown: 4
- Bullets or numbered steps: 4
- Short enough for extraction: 4
- Structured separators: 3
- Readable density: 4

The dimension is capped at 15 points.

## Long-Term Goal

Create measurable GEO ranking signals.
